Toward the Review of Materiality
In light of changes in social issues and the environment surrounding the business, DENSO is updating the materiality established in 2018.
After comprehensively extracting social issues of high interest to the international community and stakeholders, we identified opportunities and risks from the two perspectives of “the impact of DENSO’s business activities on society”
and “importance to DENSO,” and conducted a quantitative evaluation that also factored in the time axis.

In examining the materiality proposals, we incorporated the opinions and expectations for DENSO received through dialogue with key stakeholders representing the value chain,
including customers, suppliers, investors, employees, and local communities, and finalized them. After that, they were discussed at the management level
and approved by the Board of Directors. Currently, targets are being set with the materiality positioned as the premise of the next medium-term management plan, and each department and
region is formulating activity plans.
Going forward,
we will review the materiality every year based on changes in the situation surrounding sustainability, and twice a year,
at sustainability meetings, we will check the progress of activities toward achieving each materiality.
Ensuring comprehensiveness
Extraction of social issues
Referring to the SDGs, World Economic Forum (Davos) global risks, sustainability disclosure frameworks and laws and regulations, and ESG rating agency evaluation items,
we extracted economic, environmental, and social issues. A total of 116 areas were set as evaluation targets.
Reflecting DENSO’s uniqueness
Extraction of the company’s key strategies and issues
In light of the increasing complexity of the business environment, we listed DENSO’s initiatives, including themes that should be promoted company-wide and themes for which crisis response capabilities should be upgraded,
and added a total of 22 areas as candidates for evaluation.
Evaluation
Implementation of quantitative evaluation
For each evaluation target, we identified opportunities and risks for DENSO and established evaluation criteria from the two perspectives of impact on society and importance to DENSO.
For impact on society, we scored severity (scale, scope, and remediability) and likelihood; for importance for the company, we scored financial impact and likelihood,
and set the materiality proposals.
Confirming expectations from society
Dialogue with internal and external stakeholders
We held dialogues with representatives of value chain stakeholders and received opinions on the materiality proposals and expectations for DENSO.
As a result, we changed the quantitative evaluation for three themes.
Finalization Management-level discussion and approval
The materiality proposals revised in consideration of stakeholder opinions were discussed and approved by the Management Council and the Board of Directors.
