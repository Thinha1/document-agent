(2) Cash flows
① Cash flows
With respect to cash flows, cash and cash equivalents (hereinafter, “funds”) increased by 602.7 billion yen from operating activities, decreased by 363.7 billion yen from investing activities, and decreased by 400.1 billion yen from financing activities. As a result, funds at the end of the current consolidated fiscal year decreased by 134.0 billion yen compared with the previous consolidated fiscal year, to 733.9 billion yen.
Funds provided by operating activities were 602.7 billion yen, compared with 395.6 billion yen in the previous fiscal year, an increase of 207.1 billion yen. This increase was due to a 72.1 billion yen increase in income before income taxes compared with the previous fiscal year, as well as a decrease of 193.6 billion yen in the increase in inventories, and other factors.
Funds used in investing activities were 363.7 billion yen, compared with 301.6 billion yen in the previous fiscal year, an increase of 62.1 billion yen. This increase was due to a 24.2 billion yen increase in expenditures for the acquisition of tangible fixed assets, and other factors.
Funds used in financing activities were 400.1 billion yen, compared with 159.5 billion yen in the previous fiscal year, an increase of 240.6 billion yen. This increase was due to a 139.5 billion yen decrease in the net change in short-term borrowings, as well as a 60.0 billion yen increase in expenditures for the redemption of corporate bonds, and other factors.
Acquisitions of tangible fixed assets in the current consolidated fiscal year increased 7.2% from 336.4 billion yen in the previous consolidated fiscal year, to 360.6 billion yen. This increase was due to promoting investment while balancing strengthened allocation to priority areas and disciplined business operations.

With respect to capital resources and liquidity, the basic policy of the consolidated companies is to fund working capital and capital expenditure mainly from internal funds and to procure funds through borrowings or issuance of corporate bonds as necessary.

6 [Research and development activities]
Under the DENSO Group 2030 Long-term Policy, with the slogan “We want to deliver a future of smiles to the Earth, society, and all people,” the Group declares that it will contribute to creating a society filled with smiles by maximizing the value provided through “Environment” and “Safety and Security,” as well as by providing new value that earns “empathy” from society.
In the “Environment” field, the Group is conducting technology development in three areas—“mobility products,” “energy use,” and “manufacturing”—with the aim of achieving carbon neutrality. The Company has developed proprietary SiC power semiconductors with a trench MOS structure that achieve both high breakdown voltage and low on-resistance, significantly reducing power loss due to heat generation. Recently, the Company mass-produced an inverter using these SiC semiconductors (a product that drives and controls electric vehicle motors), and it was adopted in the “RZ,” LEXUS’s first dedicated battery electric vehicle (BEV) model released in March 2023. For mass production, the Company also developed material quality improvement technology to enable stable production at automotive quality. The Company is also expanding electrification technology beyond automobiles, and in May 2022 it was decided that an electric aircraft motor jointly developed with Honeywell International Inc. of the United States would be adopted for the aircraft of Lilium N.V. of Germany, which is developing an electric vertical take-off and landing aircraft. The Company also developed products for residential storage battery systems, and in the “Ouchi Kyuden System” (home power supply system) of Toyota Motor Corporation (hereinafter, “Toyota Motor”), for which orders started in June 2022, the following were adopted: a power supply adapter that links with the vehicle, a computer installed in the storage battery system, a dedicated smartphone application, and a storage battery system server that collects information on the storage battery system. As an example of hydrogen energy use, from March 2023, DENSO Fukushima Corporation, with the support of the New Energy and Industrial Technology Development Organization (NEDO), a national research and development agency, began joint demonstration with Toyota Motor Corporation of the production and use of green hydrogen within a plant. The aim is to promote carbon neutrality and build a local production for local consumption model for hydrogen.
In the “Safety and Security” field, the Company widened the detection angle of image sensors used in driver assistance in order to recognize pedestrians and bicycles crossing the vehicle’s path earlier. It is said that sudden entries from the roadside account for approximately 70% of traffic accidents involving children aged 12 or younger (Public Interest Incorporated Foundation, Institute for Traffic Accident Research and Data Analysis, “Traffic Statistics, 2019 Edition”). While a wider angle enables detection of more objects, it also increases detections that do not lead to actual danger. By using artificial intelligence (AI) motion estimation and other methods to identify danger, the Company achieved both the benefits of a wider angle and practical usability. It was newly adopted in some grades of the SUBARU “Outback” (North American specifications) announced in April 2022 and in the company’s “Crosstrek” (Japanese specifications) unveiled in September. As another example, in October 2022
From [month], we have begun a demonstration experiment in Toyota City, Aichi Prefecture, aimed at supporting safe driving by elderly people (Toyota City's traffic fatality zero
public-private partnership project “Jiko Zero Daisakusen,” which aims for zero traffic fatalities. Jointly with the Toyota Mobility Foundation, Tokio Marine & Nichido Fire Insurance
Co., Ltd., and the Graduate School of Frontier Sciences, The University of Tokyo). AI is used to analyze video and other data inside and outside the vehicle collected from drive recorders,
and using an AI driving diagnosis system that provides advice for safe driving, we will verify a method to effectively
reduce the accident risk of elderly people.

As an example of initiatives to create new value that gains public empathy, while utilizing mobility-related
assets such as technologies and products cultivated by the DENSO Group, and aiming to develop new services that help solve regional issues and improve convenience in daily life,
we launched the “DENSO OPEN INNOVATION PROJECT” in May 2022 and began broadly recruiting partner
companies. One recruitment theme is regional revitalization utilizing a regional information dissemination system. The system “Life Vision” delivers municipal and regional
information to local residents via smartphones, tablets, etc., and is based on the concepts of “anyone,” “easy,”
and “reliable,” and has been introduced in 65 municipalities to date. Beyond information distribution, as a platform function for solving diverse regional issues such as disaster prevention, welfare, education, communication,
mobility, etc., we aim to expand service functions that support daily life.
The other recruitment theme is use of identity verification combined with QR codes. DENSO
WAVE INC. holds the technology “Face Recognition SQRC,” which converts facial feature points into QR codes, and we aim to create new solutions and
markets. By storing facial information in QR codes, in situations where prevention of impersonation is required,
offline, low-cost, safe and secure identity verification can be performed. Going forward, as a mobility company, manufacturer, and QR code
inventor, to further demonstrate our expertise, we will analyze society as five flows: “people flow,” “logistics flow,” “energy flow,” “resource flow,” and “data flow,”
and create what is lacking through technology development and social collaboration.

accounted for 461,264 million yen, the North America segment 29,602 million yen, the Europe segment 13,432 million yen, the Asia segment 16,267 million yen, and
other 1,050 million yen. The Japan segment accounted for approximately 88% and plays a central role in research and development activities.
